About The Desi Corporate Girl

Welcome to my corner of the internet!

Hi, I'm Aanchal!

I'm a corporate professional, avid learner, and the voice behind The Desi Corporate Girl. This page is my space to share the lessons, insights, and real experiences from navigating the corporate world – the good, the challenging, and everything in between.

My Journey

Like many of you, my corporate journey started with equal parts excitement and uncertainty. Fresh out of college, I was eager to make my mark but had no idea what I was getting myself into. I learned quickly that success in the corporate world isn't just about technical skills or working hard (though both are important!).

It's about understanding workplace dynamics, building meaningful relationships, communicating effectively, and constantly evolving. These "soft skills" that nobody really teaches you? They're actually the hard skills that make or break careers.

Why I Started This Page

Early in my career, I wished there was someone who could break down the unwritten rules of corporate life – someone who shared honest advice without the corporate jargon. I wanted to hear from someone who understood the unique challenges of being a young professional trying to find their way.

So, I decided to become that voice for others. The Desi Corporate Girl is my attempt to:

  • Share practical advice that you can actually apply in your career
  • Demystify corporate culture and help you navigate it with confidence
  • Create a community where we can learn from each other's experiences
  • Celebrate wins and learn from challenges together
  • Advocate for authenticity in professional spaces

What You'll Find Here

This page covers everything from career growth strategies and leadership development to work-life balance and personal branding. I write about:

  • Practical tips for advancing your career
  • Navigating workplace challenges and politics
  • Building confidence and executive presence
  • Developing leadership skills at any level
  • Maintaining work-life balance and well-being
  • Real stories from my own corporate journey

My goal is simple: to provide content that's honest, actionable, and relevant to your everyday corporate experience.

My Philosophy

I believe that you don't have to compromise your authenticity to succeed in corporate. You don't have to fit into a rigid mold or pretend to be someone you're not. The most successful (and happiest) professionals I know are those who've figured out how to bring their whole selves to work.

I also believe that career growth is a marathon, not a sprint. There will be setbacks, learning curves, and moments of doubt – and that's all part of the journey. The key is to stay curious, keep learning, and be intentional about the career you're building.

Beyond the Page

When I'm not working or writing, you'll find me:

  • Reading everything from business books to fiction
  • Trying out new recipes (with varying degrees of success!)
  • Exploring new coffee shops
  • Spending time with family and friends
  • Planning my next travel adventure

Let's Connect!

This page is as much about community as it is about content. I'd love to hear your stories, challenges, and wins. Whether you're just starting your corporate journey or you're years into it, there's always something we can learn from each other.

Feel free to reach out through the contact page or connect with me on social media. I try to respond to every message, though it might take me a bit during busy work weeks!

Thank you for being here. I'm excited to be part of your career journey.
